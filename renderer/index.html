How this works

This app will read your replays as you ladder (or just game in general) and tell you how you played.

  1. You should start by putting in your ladder IDs below (you'll need to enter all the ones you play on so it knows who you are).
  2. Then go down to the training section and tell it to check your replays. This will review your replays so it knows what your averages are. Please note that this can take a while, and might slow down your PC depening on it's specs. I recommend running this off stream, ideally while you get up or work on something else.
  3. Then take the overlay link and put it in OBS — and usually 5-10s after your game (depending on length) it'll pop up with your grade!

I also added a feature that I use personally that automates Twitch predictions. You can use this if you like, but isn't needed if you don't want it.

Put it in OBS

Add a Browser source in OBS, paste this URL, and set the size to 1920 × 1080.

The overlay is blank until a game finishes — that is normal. Use Review a past replay below to put a card on screen right now.

    Grade against your own play

    Out of the box the grades compare you with the player the app was tuned on, so they can read harshly or generously for you. Training reviews your past games and rebuilds the grade boundaries from your own numbers — a normal game for you becomes a C, your best become As. It all happens on this PC; nothing is uploaded.
