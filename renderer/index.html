Your details

Your in-game name(s) add every account you play on

This is the one thing that cannot be detected automatically. A replay records the account names that played, but nothing on your PC says which of them is you — so without this, a game still gets recorded, just with no win/loss and no grade.

    StarCraft folder

    Where StarCraft is installed. The replay is re-simulated using the real game data from here, so it has to be the folder containing Data.

    Replay file LastReplay.rep

    StarCraft overwrites this one file after every game, which is what triggers a review.

    Replay folder for training, below

    Searched, including subfolders like AutoSave, when calibrating grading to your play.

    Advanced
    Port

    Only change this if something else on your PC already uses it. Changing it restarts the overlay server, so update the URL in OBS to match.

    Grade against your own play

    Out of the box the grades compare you with the player the app was tuned on, so they can read harshly or generously for you. Training reviews your past games and rebuilds the grade boundaries from your own numbers — a normal game for you becomes a C, your best become As. It all happens on this PC; nothing is uploaded.

    Takes a minute or two for 100 games.
